The problem

Estate work is broken — and the smaller the firm, the worse it gets.

01

The same data, six times.

The deceased's ID gets retyped onto J294, J192, J243, J155, the L&D heading, and the SARS REV 267. Each form is its own typing exercise.

02

SARS Estate Duty arithmetic.

Section 4(q), Section 4A rollover, the R30m / R3.5m thresholds. One mistake and SARS sends a query letter that delays finalisation by months.

03

Software that costs more than the work.

Per-seat licensing at R2,200/month doesn't fit a sole practitioner with an unpredictable matter pipeline. Most small firms run on Excel, WhatsApp, and luck.
